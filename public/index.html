Ever wonder why Jacksonville is worth a visit?

Jacksonville is the largest city in the contiguous United States by land area, with palm-lined waterfront, the St. Johns River running through downtown, and beaches a short drive away.

This is a Jewish social getaway hosted by Chabad of Town Center Jacksonville. It is built around a full Shabbos, time with friends, and room to pick your own weekend.

Everything else is taken care of.

Your meals and your room are included. You show up, and the weekend is planned.
